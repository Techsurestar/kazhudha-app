Kazhudhaa Complete Rules

1. The Deal & Goal

Played with 4 players and a standard 52-card deck (no jokers). The player holding the Ace of Spades (A♠) leads the first trick. The ultimate goal is to shed all cards from your hand before your opponents.

2. Card Power Ranking

Cards rank strictly in descending order: Ace (Highest) > King > Queen > Jack > 10 > 9 > 8 > 7 > 6 > 5 > 4 > 3 > 2 (Lowest). This hierarchy is identical across all four suits.

3. Following the Suit

Each subsequent player must follow the lead suit if they hold at least one card of that suit. If everyone follows suit, the player with the highest card of that suit wins the trick and leads the next turn. All cards in the trick are cleared.

4. The Strike ("Vettu")

If a player has no cards in the lead suit, they can strike by playing any card from another suit. This is called a Vettu. The trick ends immediately, and all cards in the trick are dumped onto the hand of the player who played the highest card of the original lead suit! That player must then lead the next trick.

5. Scoring & The Kazhudhaa

  • 1st to empty hand: 20 points
  • 2nd to empty hand: 15 points
  • 3rd to empty hand: 10 points
  • Last player (Kazhudhaa): 0 points

Matches continue across rounds until at least one player scores 100 points or more. The player with the highest total points wins the game!
